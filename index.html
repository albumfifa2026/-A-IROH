What is (A)IROH?

(A)IROH is a software studio focused on building simple digital tools that solve real everyday problems. We believe many daily frustrations can be solved with thoughtful technology designed around people.

Our First Project

Every World Cup, millions of people collect sticker albums. And everyone faces the same problem: duplicates and random trades.

Track Your Stickers

Keep a digital record of every sticker you already have.

Find Duplicates

Automatically detect which stickers you can trade.

Smart Trading

Find people who have the stickers you need.

Finish Faster

Complete your album with fewer wasted trades.

Who is this for?

Anyone collecting the World Cup album: kids, collectors, football fans, or anyone who wants to finish the album faster.
