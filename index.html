Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how (A)IROH ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Álbum Mundial 2026: Stickers mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to our servers.

If you choose to enable analytics during onboarding, we collect:

  • Anonymous usage data (screens visited, features used, session duration)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language settings
  • App performance metrics (e.g. crash reports)

This data is collected anonymously through Firebase Analytics (Google) and cannot be used to identify you personally. You can disable analytics at any time from within the App.

2. Camera Permission

The App requests access to your device's camera solely to scan QR codes for the sticker trading feature. We do not capture, store, or transmit any photos or video. Camera access is only active when you initiate a QR scan.

3. Data Storage

All your sticker collection data (stickers marked, duplicates, trade history) is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

4. Trading Feature

The QR-based trading feature works peer-to-peer between devices. No trade data is sent to or stored on our servers. The only information shared during a trade is the QR code content exchanged directly between two devices.

5. Third-Party Services

If analytics is enabled, data is processed by Google Firebase Analytics. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
